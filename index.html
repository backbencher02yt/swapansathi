স্বপন সাথী ট্রাস্টের বিশ্বাস

Swapan Sathi Trust is founded on the belief that every life deserves hope, dignity and opportunity.

We support cancer research and education, empower persons with disabilities, and preserve our rich cultural heritage, so that our society grows more compassionate, inclusive and rooted in its own soil.

আমাদের তিনটি লক্ষ্য

Our three missions

Cancer research, awareness & education

ক্যান্সার গবেষণা, সচেতনতা ও শিক্ষা

We help people understand cancer early and back the research and education that give patients and families a better chance.

Dignity & independence for Divyangjan

দিব্যাঙ্গজনের মর্যাদা, শিক্ষা ও স্বনির্ভরতা

We stand with persons with disabilities, supporting their education, dignity and independence in everyday village life.

Reviving our culture & heritage

ঐতিহ্য, সংস্কৃতি ও মূল্যবোধের পুনরুজ্জীবন

We keep our folk traditions, crafts, songs and values alive and hand them on to the next generation.

গ্রামের মাটি থেকে, গ্রামের মানুষের জন্য

Our work begins where the road turns to a mud path: in courtyards, under banyan trees, beside the village pond. We believe lasting change is built slowly, together, and close to home.

সবার সাথে

Every neighbour is part of the work, from elders to children.

সেবার মনে

শিকড়ের টানে
